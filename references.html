The research behind it

BodiedGPT builds on published work about language-model agents. Here is each paper, what it found, and what BodiedGPT takes from it.

Most important

Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior
Joon Sung Park, Joseph C. O'Brien, Carrie J. Cai, Meredith Ringel Morris, Percy Liang, Michael S. Bernstein · 2023 · arXiv:2304.03442

25 language-model agents live in a small town called "Smallville". They remember, reflect and plan, and believable social behaviour emerges, such as news and invitations spreading from one agent to another. Used in BodiedGPT: the memory stream, importance scores, retrieval by recency + importance + relevance, reflection triggered by accumulated importance, and interviewing agents without affecting them.

Agents that think and act

ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models
Shunyu Yao, Jeffrey Zhao, Dian Yu, et al. · 2022 · arXiv:2210.03629

Interleaving written reasoning with actions makes language-model agents more capable and easier to understand. Used in BodiedGPT: every decision pairs a "thought" with an action or intention, and both are shown in the observation log.

Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning
Noah Shinn, Federico Cassano, Edward Berman, et al. · 2023 · arXiv:2303.11366

Agents improve by reflecting in words on what happened and keeping those reflections in memory. Used in BodiedGPT: clear feedback on what actually happened after each wish, and stored insights. These were what broke the loops small models get stuck in.

Embodiment and memory

Voyager: An Open-Ended Embodied Agent with Large Language Models
Guanzhi Wang, Yuqi Xie, Yunfan Jiang, et al. · 2023 · arXiv:2305.16291

A language model plays Minecraft open-endedly, exploring and learning skills with no fixed goal. Relevant to BodiedGPT: the same question of what an agent does with an open world and no instructions, here with a human body and a much simpler world.

MemGPT: Towards LLMs as Operating Systems
Charles Packer, Sarah Wooders, Kevin Lin, et al. · 2023 · arXiv:2310.08560

Managing an agent's limited context like an operating system manages memory: a small working memory plus a larger store it can pull from. Used in BodiedGPT: the split between a short window of recent events, a running summary, and older memories retrieved on demand.

Many agents

Project Sid: Many-agent simulations toward AI civilization
Altera.AL et al. · 2024 · arXiv:2411.00114

Large numbers of language-model agents in Minecraft form roles, rules and culture. Relevant to BodiedGPT: a direction for the future, scaling from two subjects to small societies.

Tools

  • Ollama: runs open models locally, with structured (JSON schema) output.
  • OpenAI API: cloud models, structured outputs and embeddings (text-embedding-3-small).
  • Three.js: the 3D island in the browser.
